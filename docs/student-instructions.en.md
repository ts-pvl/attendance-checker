# How to check in

## Once: link your Telegram

1. Open the personal link you were given on your phone (or scan the QR on your slip).
2. The school bot opens — tap **Start**.
3. The bot answers "Linked as <your name>". Done.

Never share your link: whoever opens it first becomes you.

## Every time you sit down at a PC

1. Log in — the browser opens the page with the QR code.
2. Scan the code with your phone camera → Telegram opens → tap **Start** if asked.
3. Wait for **"Checked in at PC-17 ✅"**.

The code changes every 30 seconds — scan the one on the screen now.

## Leaving

Nothing to do: when you log out, or the PC is idle for 30 minutes, your visit
closes at your last activity and the bot sends you a message. To record the
exact time you leave, send the bot `/checkout`.

Only one student is checked in at a PC at a time. If someone else scans the
code on the PC you are using, your visit ends and the bot tells you. Scan
again if you are still there.

`/status` shows your current visit and today's hours.

## What is recorded

Only your name, Telegram ID, arrival and departure times, and staff
corrections. Staff can see your attended hours. We do not track what you do on the PC.
